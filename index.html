Research

Chimeric RNAs and gene fusions in cancer

I identify fusion transcripts in RNA-seq data and test what they do in cells. These include PLEKHB2::FAM168B in breast invasive carcinoma, FAM168B::ANKRD42 in prostate adenocarcinoma, and SOX10::FAM168A in lower-grade glioma. All three arise through intergenic trans-splicing at the RNA level rather than genomic rearrangement.

The dual role of FAM168B

FAM168B (also called MANI) sits at the crossroads of cancer progression and neurite-outgrowth inhibition. I use GFP-tagged overexpression in MCF7, PC3, HeLa, PC12 and SH-SY5Y cells to measure its effect on the cell cycle, apoptosis and signaling, by western blot and flow cytometry.

Cancer genomics and biomarker discovery

Pan-cancer analysis of TCGA expression, mutation and survival data, with a focus on PRAD and BRCA, carried out and visualized in R. Earlier work covered mass-spectrometry proteomics, metabolomics and miRNA profiling in intervertebral disc degeneration.
